Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does pool service cost?

It depends on the size of the pool, whether there's a spa, and how often you want service. We look at the pool first, give you a price before anything starts, and you decide from there.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. The initial consultation and estimate are free.

How often should my pool be serviced?

Weekly service is the standard for most Southern California pools — it keeps chemistry stable and stops algae before it starts. Spas and hot tubs can be scheduled on their own cycle.

Are chemicals included?

We'll walk you through what's included in your plan up front, so there are no surprise add-ons on the bill.

Can you fix a green or cloudy pool?

Yes. We shock, brush, run and clean the filter, and rebalance the water until it's clear, then keep it there with regular service.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Carson and serve Los Angeles County, Orange County, and the South Bay, including Torrance, Long Beach, Redondo Beach, Huntington Beach, and Irvine.
